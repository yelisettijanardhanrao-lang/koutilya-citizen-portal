One & Same Person Affidavit

Online preparation of a single affidavit

Use: This affidavit is for situations where two or more document records contain name variations that refer to the same person. The receiving authority may require a different prescribed form, certificate, Gazette process or other evidence. Verify the current requirement before execution.

Supporting documents: Keep the documents showing each name variation ready for submission to the concerned authority.
